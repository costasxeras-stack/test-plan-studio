How the numbers are worked out

    
    
      
  • Sample size for conversion rates uses the standard two-proportion formula with unequal allocation (Fleiss). For averages it uses the normal approximation with your standard deviation.
    • 
      
  • With more than one variant, the significance level is split across comparisons (Bonferroni), which is simple and slightly conservative.
    • 
      
  • Duration is rounded up to whole weeks, so weekday and weekend behaviour are both in the sample.
    • 
      
  • Results use a two-proportion z-test (pooled) for the p-value and an unpooled interval for the difference. Averages use a large-sample z-test (Welch standard error).
    • 
      
  • The sample ratio check is a chi-square test against your planned split. A p-value under 0.001 means the split is broken and the result should not be trusted.
